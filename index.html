How the success score works

The Student Success Score is a 0–100 composite indicator for early support planning, combining academic progress, attendance, LMS engagement, placement readiness and engagement/feedback.

Academic progress 35%Attendance 20%LMS engagement 15%Placement readiness 20%Engagement & feedback 10%
Risk flags

High: score below 55, attendance below 65%, or 2+ backlogs. Medium: score below 70 or a notable single-area gap. Low: score 70+ without a critical signal. Thresholds should be calibrated with institutional outcomes.

Use with care

This prototype uses illustrative sample data and transparent rules, not a validated predictive model. Scores support human review and should not be used alone for consequential decisions.
